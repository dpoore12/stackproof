# StackProof — thesis and evidence

Research date: 2026-09-03. Every figure below is from a named source or a
live API pull on that date. Items marked ASSUMPTION are not measured.

## The one-sentence version

Buy and run expensive business software, publish thousands of specific
measured findings about it, be the source AI assistants cite when someone
asks which tool to buy, and collect 20–30% of the subscription every month
for a year or two.

## Why affiliate, and why this shape

**The distribution channel changed under everyone.**

- AI Overviews now appear on 83% of Google queries; position-one organic CTR
  is 58% lower when one is present. Informational search as a traffic
  source is collapsing.
- But AI-referred traffic converts at **1.66%** to sign-up versus **0.15%**
  from traditional search — 11×. In March 2025 AI traffic converted 38%
  *worse* than other channels; by March 2026 it converted 42% *better*. An
  80-point swing in twelve months.
- **67.82% of sources cited in AI Overviews do not rank in Google's top 10**
  for the same query. LLMs select passages, not pages. Twenty years of
  accumulated domain authority does not transfer to the new channel.
- No single domain exceeds ~5% of citations on any platform; the rest is a
  long tail across thousands of domains. Not winner-take-all.
- Platforms cite differently: ChatGPT 47.9% encyclopedic sources;
  Perplexity 46.7% Reddit plus review platforms (G2, Yelp); Google AI
  Overviews 23.3% YouTube/multimodal.

**So the product is a citation surface, not a website:** short, specific,
dated, numeric, attributable passages, across a long tail of narrow
decision questions. That is the unit an assistant lifts.

**Price point: optimise retention, not commission rate.** 50% of a $10/mo
tool at 2-month retention is $10 per referral. 25% of a $100/mo tool at
9-month retention is $225. The lower rate pays 22× more. Chase software a
business cannot rip out: payroll, accounting.

## Mechanics a newcomer would not know to ask about

1. **Disintermediation.** OpenAI launched Instant Checkout (buy inside
   ChatGPT) in September 2025 and retired it in March 2026 after five
   months; ~12 of Shopify's merchants ever went live. ChatGPT now does
   discovery and hands off to the merchant — the affiliate shape. Google
   (UCP) and Perplexity (Instant Buy) still pursue agentic checkout, and
   OpenAI's first commerce partners are consumer retailers. Nobody buys
   payroll in a chat window; B2B SaaS is the least exposed segment.
2. **Program acceptance is gated.** Impact requires a media-kit PDF with
   traffic data; a site with 50k monthly visitors is still rejected if the
   traffic is off-intent. Earn traffic first, apply second.
3. **"Lifetime recurring" is mostly 12–24 months.** 71% of SaaS programs
   pay recurring, typically 20–30% for 12 months, 10–15% for months 13–24,
   then declining. 19% pay first year only. 10% are one-time bounties.
4. **Clawbacks are standard** on refunds and early churn.
5. **FTC Consumer Review Rule** prohibits AI-generated content presented as
   authentic consumer experience. Generic affiliate disclosure is not
   enough; an AI-specific disclosure is required. TikTok's Creator Rewards
   bans AI content from monetisation outright. The compliant strategy and
   the citable strategy are the same one: actually buy it, actually run it,
   report real measured numbers with dates.

## Revenue, honestly

Benchmarks: SaaS EPC $1.20–$3.50; affiliate conversion in high-intent SaaS
4–8%; visitor-to-trial 2.1–7.1%; trial-to-paid 12–28%; high-ACV B2B flat
commissions $200–500 per qualified deal; median B2B SaaS commission 20%.

High-ticket path to $1M/yr: $1,000,000 ÷ $350 = 2,857 conversions/yr
(238/mo) → at 4–8% conversion, 3,000–6,000 merchant clicks/mo → at ~15%
click-out, **20,000–40,000 highly targeted visitors/mo**.

Timeline benchmarks: first sale in months 3–6; SaaS 8–16 weeks to first
commission plus 30–60 days to pay out; most affiliates earn $0–1,000/mo in
year one and take 12–24 months to reach $1,000/mo consistently.

## The load-bearing unknown

AI referral traffic is **under 1% of total visits even at large sites**
(ChatGPT sends 92.4% of all AI referrals and is still under 1%). It grew
157.7% week-on-week after a May 2026 product update, from a small base.
11× conversion on 1% of traffic is real but not a year-one engine. This is
a 24–36 month compounding asset bought for ~$10k/year of software, and the
bet is that a channel where incumbency does not transfer becomes 10–20% of
commercial discovery by 2028.

## Cost base

$500–800/month of software, staggered five or six tools at a time for
60–90 days each. Free trials cover setup and interface findings; they do not
cover the findings worth citing — real cost at scale, support response on a
paid ticket, renewal behaviour, and whether you can get your data out when
you leave. Payroll cannot be tested without a real employee and real
filings; it needs the account holder's entity.
