Page description: "Plans from $30/month (USD). Choose Pay as you go,
Startup, or Enterprise for your tutoring business."

Three tiers, each labelled "Starting from" rather than a flat
guaranteed price, and differentiated mainly by payment-processing rate
and support/feature tier rather than by a stated tutor or staff count:

Pay as you go — "Starting from $30/month." "For tutoring companies
starting out." Standard card fees 3.85%. 3 months of priority phone
support, 1 hour platform optimisation call, 1 hour training session.

Startup — "Starting from $80/month." "For scaling tutoring companies."
Standard card fees 3.5%. 3 months of priority phone support, 1 hour
platform optimisation call, 3 x 1 hour training sessions. Adds
customisable/configurable dashboard, customisable email design, user &
report exports.

Enterprise — "Starting from $240/month." "For large-scale tutoring
companies." "Competitive rates for high-volume merchants." Unlimited
technical support including integrations, unlimited chat support, API
& custom integrations, custom JS/CSS.

No tutor-count, student-count, or other seat figure is stated for any
tier in the static text; the page instead offers an interactive
calculator ("Want to estimate which the best price plan would be for
you...") to recommend a plan. All prices exclusive of tax.
