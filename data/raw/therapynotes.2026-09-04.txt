Three tiers, confirmed via raw page text:

Solo — "$69/month for one user." "Great for solo practitioners or new
providers who are just getting started."

Group — "$79/month for the first clinician" + "$50/mo per additional
clinician." "Free non-clinical staff" (non-clinical staff accounts do
not count toward the per-clinician charge).

Enterprise (20+ Users) — identical formula: "$79/month for the first
clinician" + "$50/mo per additional clinician." Adds "Free non-clinical
staff" and "Designated account manager" — i.e. Enterprise is the same
pricing formula as Group, just the volume tier (20+ users) at which a
dedicated account manager is included; not modeled as a separately
priced tier.

Add-ons (not standalone): Premium Telehealth+ "$15/mo per clinician,"
TherapyFuel+ "$40/mo per clinician," Electronic Billing "14¢ per
electronic claim."
