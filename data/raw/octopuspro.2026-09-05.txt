Title: "OctopusPro Pricing - Field Service Management Software"

Has a dedicated gutter-cleaning landing page: "Gutter Cleaning
Software | Scheduling, Quotes & Invoices" at
octopuspro.com/gutter-cleaning-software/ — extremely extensive,
genuinely gutter-cleaning-specific content (gutter cleaner-to-job
matching, digital intake forms/checklists/property profiles, gutter
supplies and equipment stock control, quote-to-booking workflow,
distance-based travel fees). OctopusPro maintains near-identical
dedicated pages for dozens of trades.

Starter — "For owner-operators" — "$36.25 per month" — "Or $348 per
year (save 20%)" [=$29/mo] — "Includes 1 Admin + 1 Fieldworker." No
additional-seat overage disclosed for this tier.

Established — "Most popular" — "For growing teams" — Monthly billing:
"$60 USD per admin seat/month" + "$6 USD per fieldworker seat/month."
Annual billing: "$600 USD per admin/year (-17%)" [=$50/mo] + "$60 USD
per fieldworker/year (-17%)" [=$5/mo]. "Included Seats: Unlimited
users" — i.e., no seat cap, but every admin and every fieldworker
seat is billed individually; there is no bundled free seat of either
type.

Unlimited (Users plan) — Per the pricing FAQ: "Unlimited Users Plan –
Platform Licence $99.00" per month, no cap on user seats. Usage-based
fee on top, customer's choice of either "Booked Jobs ($1 per job with
at least one fieldworker assigned)" or "Revenue Commission (2% of
invoiced value per month)" — switchable in Settings. Sample invoice
line shown: "Unlimited Users Plan – Platform Licence $99.00" plus
either "Booked Jobs (245 x $1) $245.00" or "Revenue Commission (2% of
$47,850 invoiced) $957.00." The $99 platform fee itself is a flat,
unlimited-seat number; the usage-based add-on is not a per-seat charge
and is not captured as a number here.
