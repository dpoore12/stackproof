"Plans that work for engineers, IT, security, and home users."
Personal — "$0 for up to 6 users" — Unlimited devices, 50 tagged resources included.
Standard — "$8 per user, per month" — Everything in Personal plus: Basic Tailscale SSH (up to 5 hosts), Kubernetes ingress/egress (See platform extensions), SSO with any IdP, basic user roles, 1,000 ephemeral-resource minutes/month, 300 ACL groups (per compare table), basic customer support.
Premium — "$18 per user, per month" — Everything in Standard plus: Advanced Tailscale SSH, Funnel, device posture integrations (MDM/EDR/XDR/geolocation), just-in-time access, advanced user roles, SCIM provisioning, webhooks, configuration audit logs, priority customer support.
Enterprise — Custom — Everything in Premium plus custom account sizes, custom MSAs & SLAs, pay by invoice, dedicated professional services.
Add-ons: additional tagged resources $1/month each (50 included free per month); additional ephemeral-resource minutes contact sales; multiple tailnets contact sales; premium support/dedicated services contact sales.
FAQ: "Does Tailscale offer a free trial?" — "Yes! Business customers can enjoy a 14-day free trial of the product with no user limit... Personal users have access to the free tier plan, which will be free forever."
